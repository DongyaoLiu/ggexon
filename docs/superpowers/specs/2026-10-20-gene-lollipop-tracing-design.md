# Traceable gene lollipops

User approved clearer stems and ordered head groups, preserving exact genomic anchors, common bp geometry, native grey context genes, VR lane, and existing MHC colors/status. No VR zoom is requested in this iteration.

Arrange consecutive display-ordered genes into fixed-width columns read top to bottom and then left to right. Search for the smallest feasible row count with bounded horizontal movement for every head; all members of a column share its x centre. Reserve a physical gutter to its left. Every gene has a unique lane within that gutter, ordered left to right for top to bottom heads. From its exact genomic x at the common baseline a leader runs vertically through a short anchor tick, fans through a clear band to its lane, rises beside the head column and enters the head at its left midpoint. This ordering prevents proper leader crossings and avoids other heads. Identical genomic anchors may share a starting segment and are ordered stably.

The grey context gene bodies occupy y=-0.5..0 beneath the exact anchor ticks, with the shared VR lane below them; this keeps the fan band clear. Keep existing compact packing as an option; use the new column layout in the MHC renderer. Stems automatically use a darker shade of the head fill, with thin white halos drawn beneath all colored leaders. Anchor ticks are thin and retain exact x positions. Unknown strands remain boxes; pseudogenes retain grey heads, charcoal stems and their asterisks. No backbone is introduced. Arrow widths remain schematic physical dimensions; only anchors and context models encode genomic coordinates.

Expose layout, routing-band/tick/lane sizes and stem styling as reusable geom parameters. Extend draw-time callback records with display order, column, lane, path vertices, tick height and actual stem color. Independently audit routing segments against all other heads, proper path crossings, column reading order, anchor invariance, labels, bounds and per-device physical layout. Export the previous figure for comparison and render the polished full figure plus native scientific detail views of human/ape VR3. Increase height if required to retain font/head size and readable routing clearances.
